Questions

Does a custom loop need upkeep?

Very little. Top up the reservoir once a year and swap the coolant every two years. The glass makes the level easy to check.

What if something leaks?

Every loop is pressure-tested for 24 hours before it ships. If a fitting ever weeps, we cover the part and the repair for three years.

Can I upgrade it later?

Yes. Every assembly comes out with a screwdriver, and the loop has quick-disconnects at the graphics card and the processor.

How loud is it?

About 24 dB at the desk when idle and 38 dB at full load, measured one metre away.

How long does it take to arrive?

Around six weeks from your reservation: four to build and test, two to ship with the loop already filled.
